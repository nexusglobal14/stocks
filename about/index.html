About Nexus Stocks & Markets

Nexus Stocks & Markets is a free dashboard from Nexus Global Information Technology. It brings together live charts for USD/PKR, gold and oil with calculators for the questions Pakistani freelancers, exporters and gold buyers ask most often.

Where the data comes from

  • Charts and tickers are embedded from TradingView. We do not control the feeds, and they may be delayed.
  • Live exchange rates used in the calculators are fetched in your browser from open.er-api.com. They are indicative mid-market rates.
  • Tax information is a summary of publicly reported rules and is dated on each page. It is not tax advice.

What we do not do

We do not give investment, trading or tax advice, we do not sell financial products and we do not hold your money. Always check prices and rules with your bank, broker, dealer or a qualified professional.
