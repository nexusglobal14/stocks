What this calculator does

It converts the international gold price, quoted in US dollars per troy ounce, into an estimated rupee price per gram, per 10 grams and per tola. You enter the spot price and the USD/PKR rate, choose the karat, and optionally add a local premium or making charge.

The formula

Price per gram (PKR) = spot price (USD per ounce) ÷ 31.1034768 × USD/PKR rate × purity × (1 + premium %). Price per tola is the gram price multiplied by 11.6638038, and price per 10 grams is the gram price multiplied by 10.

Worked example

Suppose gold is 2,000 USD per ounce and USD/PKR is 280. Pure gold costs 2,000 ÷ 31.1035 × 280, which is about 18,004 PKR per gram. Multiply by 11.6638 and a tola of 24K gold comes to about 210,000 PKR, and 10 grams is about 180,044 PKR. For 22K, multiply by 0.9167. These are illustrative numbers, not current prices.

Why the market price may differ

  • Premiums and import costs. Local dealers add margins on top of the global price.
  • Making charges. Jewellery prices include workmanship, usually a percentage or a flat amount per tola.
  • Rate timing. The spot price and the exchange rate both move through the day, and local associations publish rates at set times.
  • Rounding conventions and the purity assumed for "pure" gold.

Useful units

  • 1 troy ounce = 31.1035 grams
  • 1 tola = 11.6638 grams
  • 1 tola is about 0.375 troy ounces

Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in a tola?

A tola used in Pakistan and India is about 11.6638 grams. A troy ounce is about 31.1035 grams.

Why does my jeweller's price differ from this calculator?

Local prices include import costs, dealer premiums, demand and, for jewellery, making charges. This tool shows the formula value from the spot price and exchange rate.

Where do I get the spot gold price?

The live chart on our home page shows OANDA:XAUUSD. Type its current USD per ounce value into the calculator. Prices can be delayed, so confirm with a dealer.

What do 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K mean?

They describe purity. 24K is about 99.9% gold, 22K about 91.7%, 21K about 87.5% and 18K about 75%. The calculator multiplies the price by the selected purity.

Is this an official rate?

No. Official and association rates are set locally and can differ. This is an educational estimate.
