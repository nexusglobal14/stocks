How to use live forex and commodity data in Pakistan

Practical notes for IT exporters, freelancers, importers and retail observers.

Watching currency and commodity moves matters if you are paid in dollars, buy imported goods or follow gold. Nexus Stocks focuses on a few high-use pairs, such as USD/PKR, AED/PKR, SAR/PKR and gold (XAU/USD), and puts them on one clean page.

1. Why USD/PKR and gold move together locally

Local gold prices are driven by two things: the international price of gold per troy ounce (XAU/USD) and the dollar-rupee exchange rate. Even when the global gold price is flat, a 1% move in USD/PKR changes the rupee price of gold by about 1%. Local rates also include premiums and dealer margins, so they will not match a pure formula. Our gold tola calculator shows the arithmetic.

2. Estimating what a foreign payment is worth in PKR

When a payment from abroad reaches your Pakistani account, the amount credited differs from the chart rate for two main reasons:

  • Bank conversion spread. Banks convert at their own rate, usually somewhat below the market reference rate. The gap varies by bank and by day. A range of roughly 0.3% to 1% is a common planning assumption, but check your own bank.
  • Export withholding tax. As reported after Budget 2026-27, IT exporters registered with the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB) pay a concessional 0.25% final tax on export proceeds, extended to June 30, 2029, while other export proceeds are generally subject to 1%. Conditions apply, so confirm with PSEB, FBR or a tax adviser. See the export tax guide.

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices on this page real-time?

The charts and tickers are provided by TradingView and show live market data, but some feeds can be delayed. Always confirm prices with your bank, exchange company or broker before transacting.

Is the USD to PKR rate here the rate my bank will give me?

No. Charts show a market reference rate. Your bank or exchange company applies its own rate and spread, and the open-market rate in Pakistan can differ.

What does the FX and remittance calculator do?

It estimates the PKR you would receive after a bank conversion spread and an export withholding tax tier. It is an estimate for planning, not a tax calculation.
