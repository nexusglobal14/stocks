What this calculator does

Enter the amount of foreign currency you expect to receive, an exchange rate, the bank's conversion spread and a tax tier. The tool multiplies the amount by the rate to get the gross value in rupees, subtracts the spread, then subtracts the withholding tax from what remains. The result is an estimate of your net PKR payout.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount you will be paid and choose the currency.
  2. Click Use live rate for a current market reference, or type the rate your bank quoted.
  3. Enter your bank's spread. A planning figure of 0.3% to 1% is common, but ask your bank.
  4. Choose the tax tier that matches your situation.
  5. Read the gross, deducted and net amounts.

Export tax in Pakistan: the basics

As reported after the federal budget for 2026-27, IT and IT-enabled services exporters registered with the Pakistan Software Export Board continue to benefit from a reduced final withholding rate of 0.25% on export proceeds, extended through June 30, 2029. Other export proceeds are generally described as subject to 1%. Sources such as Business Recorder and TechJuice have covered the extension.

Commonly cited conditions include registering with PSEB, receiving payments through approved banking channels and staying on the Active Taxpayer List by filing your annual return. Some guides also mention a rule that most export proceeds must be remitted to Pakistan. A separate income tax exemption under Section 65F has its own, stricter conditions and is not the same thing as the 0.25% regime. Because tax rules change and depend on your circumstances, treat this page as an overview and verify with PSEB, the Federal Board of Revenue or a qualified adviser.

What this tool does not include

  • Income tax on other income, provincial sales tax on services or any local-client income.
  • Payment platform fees such as Payoneer, Wise or marketplace charges, which are taken before the money reaches your bank.
  • Exact bank charges, which vary. Add them into the spread field if you know them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the export tax rate for PSEB-registered IT freelancers?

Reports on Budget 2026-27 say the concessional 0.25% final tax on IT export proceeds for PSEB-registered exporters was extended to June 30, 2029. Always confirm the current rule and your eligibility with PSEB, FBR or a tax adviser.

What rate applies if I am not registered with PSEB?

Published guidance describes 1% as the standard withholding rate on export proceeds for those not registered. Your own position can vary, so get professional advice.

Is money sent home by a family member taxed?

Home remittances sent through banking channels are generally treated differently from business export income and are commonly described as tax exempt. That is why the calculator offers a 0% option. Check with your bank for your case.

Why is the amount I receive lower than the chart rate times my dollars?

Banks convert at their own rate, so a spread is taken, and withholding tax may be deducted at source. This calculator estimates both.

Does the calculator work for EUR, GBP, AED and SAR?

Yes. Pick the currency and the tool fetches the matching live rate to PKR. You can also type your own rate, such as the one your bank quoted.
